# Look: `taxonomy-infographic`

**慎用。** 仅当 Method 本身是概念/范式分类墙（非检测骨干）。

## Design thinking

```text
Use only for taxonomy or paradigm galleries. Soft pastel cards in a grid with
short labels. Do not use for trainable pipelines — switch to stage or dual-stream.
仅用于概念分类墙；可训练管线不要用这个 look。
```

## Style keywords

taxonomy infographic pastel cards grid, short labels, minimal arrows,
academic concept map, not a training pipeline
