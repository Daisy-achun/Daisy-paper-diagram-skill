# Look: `frozen-encoder-rail`

冻结 ViT/CLIP 竖条轨 + 可训 LoRA/检测头；❄/🔥 图例一次；主轨 + 可选右侧 zoom。

## Design thinking

```text
Draw the frozen backbone as a long rail of vertical bars or a tall rounded block
with snowflakes. Attach a small peach LoRA or fire-marked head. Put train vs
infer as separate dashed boxes if both exist. Side inset only for novel module
internals, not for repeating the whole rail.
冻结骨干画成竖条轨或高圆角块+❄；旁挂小桃LoRA/🔥头；训练/推理分虚线盒；
仅新颖模块做右侧inset。
```
